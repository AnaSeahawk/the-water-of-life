## Practice

**Pathways:** oral (fresh and aged), topical (fresh and aged), nasal,
eye, ear, gargle/swish, compress, bath/soak, enema, nebulizer,
hair/scalp, vaginal wash

**Duration:** over 4 years; daily practice since 3 April 2022

**Overall:** positive

## What I observed

I had been experiencing really intense wrist pain for many years. I had
radial shortening in both my arms. I suffered with a lot of intense
wrist pain for years before and since having that surgery. I had just
done a lot of moving and heavy lifting, and my wrists were in really
bad shape.

I heard about urine therapy from Dave Murphy and instantly started to
fast. Within the second day, I was already feeling a huge shift in my
wrists — I could move them again, and I wasn't having all this pain. By
the end of the third day, it was better than back to normal. I've
continued the practice ever since. At that time I was only drinking
fresh, but since then I've done everything.

## What else was happening

Had recently switched to a vegetarian diet before starting the fast.
The fast was urine only — no other fluids for three days. No other
treatments at the time.
