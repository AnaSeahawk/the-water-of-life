## Who is responsible

My name is Ana Seahawk. I am the founder, steward, and sole data
controller for this archive. There is no organization behind it. I am
personally responsible for how your data is handled.

Contact: anaseahawk@gmail.com

---

## What is collected

When you submit the observation form, the following is collected:

- Your practice pathways, duration, and frequency
- What you observed
- Your overall experience
- What else was happening (concurrent factors)
- Any additional details you chose to share
- Your display name, if you provided one (otherwise anonymous)
- Your age range, if you provided it

Some of this information constitutes health-related data under data
protection laws (including GDPR, where it applies), including
observations about your body and any mention of medications or
treatments. You choose what to include. The only required fields are
the consent checkbox and the concurrent factors section, which can be
a single sentence.

The legal basis for processing your data is your explicit consent,
given through the consent checkbox on the observation form. For
health-related data specifically, this relies on GDPR Article 9(2)(a)
— explicit consent for specified purposes.

For audio interviews, the conversation is recorded. The recording is
used solely for transcription and is never published.

---

## What becomes public

Your observation — the formatted version of your submission — is
published in a public GitHub repository and displayed on this website.
It includes:

- Your display name (or anonymous if none provided)
- Your age range (if provided)
- Your practice details, observation, concurrent factors, and any
  additional details you chose to share

The following are **never published**:

- Audio recordings from interviews
- Any information you explicitly ask to keep private during an interview

---

## How submissions are processed

Form submissions are processed through
[Formspree](https://formspree.io), a third-party form service. When you
submit the form, your responses pass through Formspree's servers and are
delivered to my email. Formspree's own privacy policy applies to that
transmission: [formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy).

Once received, I review and format your submission by hand. The
formatted entry is added to the archive's Git repository. I then delete
the original submission from Formspree and from my email.

Audio interview recordings are stored privately on my own devices, used
only for transcription, and deleted once the transcript has been
reviewed and approved by the contributor.

---

## Who has access

- **Me** — I am the only person who sees raw form submissions, email
  addresses, and audio recordings
- **Formspree** — processes the form submission during transmission
- **The public** — sees only the published archive entry, which contains
  no email addresses or private information

---

## How long data is retained

- **Published entries** remain in the archive indefinitely. Because the
  archive uses Git version control, the history of changes is preserved
  even after an entry is modified or removed — the Git record will show
  that an entry existed and was changed or removed, though the content
  itself is removed from the current version
- **Audio recordings** (from interviews) are deleted after the transcript is reviewed and
  approved by you
- **Raw form submissions** in Formspree and email are deleted after your
  entry is formatted and published

---

## Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **Request a copy** of any data I hold about you
- **Request correction** of your published entry
- **Request removal** of your observation from the archive — removals
  are tracked in the Git history (the record shows an entry was removed,
  and why) but your content is removed from the current, publicly visible
  version of the archive
- **Withdraw consent** — if you withdraw consent, your entry will be
  removed as described above
- **Ask questions** about how your data is handled
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority if you
  believe your data has been handled improperly

For any of these, contact me at anaseahawk@gmail.com. I will respond
within 30 days.

## Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, or any
third-party monitoring. There is no advertising. The site is hosted on
GitHub Pages, which may log standard server access data (IP addresses,
request times) as part of its infrastructure — this is outside my
control. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that:
[docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Age requirement

Contributors must be 18 years of age or older. This archive does not
knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

---

## Changes to this page

If this privacy page is updated, the change will be visible in the
archive's Git history. Contributors whose entries are already published
will not be affected by changes made after their submission — your entry
was accepted under the terms that existed when you submitted it.

---

## International data transfers

Form submissions pass through Formspree, whose servers are located in
the United States. If you are submitting from outside the US, your data
crosses an international border during transmission. The published
archive is hosted on GitHub Pages, also based in the US. Both services
maintain their own data protection commitments, linked elsewhere on this
page.
