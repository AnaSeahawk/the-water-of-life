The Water of Life

Thousands of people are living this practice right now. They have been for years, for decades, for generations. No institution has been willing to collect their experiences. No trial has been conducted. No dataset exists.

So we are building it ourselves.

This is an open-source, participatory archive of first-person accounts from people who engage with auto-urine practice — in their own words, in observational language, with the full picture of their lives alongside it. No claims. No diagnoses. No prescriptions. Just the data that should have been collected a long time ago.

Why this exists

The historical record goes back five thousand years. The practice is referenced in the Damar Tantra, the Sushruta Samhita, the Hatha Yoga Pradipika, and across Ayurvedic tradition. Armstrong's The Water of Life (1944) documented decades of Western case histories.

And yet: as of August 2026, no controlled clinical trial on auto-urine therapy in humans has been conducted. The institutional position is that "no scientific evidence supports" the practice. But that is not a conclusion reached after investigation. It is a refusal to investigate.

The data is not missing. It is everywhere — in the lives of practitioners who have been quietly doing this work for years. No one with the authority to make it "count" has been willing to collect it. The file is empty not because there is nothing to put in it, but because no one has opened it.

This archive opens it.

Contribute your experience

If you are living this practice, your experience belongs in the record. Not because it proves anything — but because it exists, and right now, no one is counting it.

Every entry that goes into this archive is something that did not exist before. Every story shared is one more piece of a record that is long overdue.

  • Request an interview — a recorded conversation, transcribed and formatted into an archive entry
