The Water of Life

People are already living this practice. Every day, they notice things — sometimes remarkable, sometimes ordinary, sometimes difficult, sometimes nothing at all. Most of those observations disappear.

The Water of Life is a place to leave them.

What did you do? What did you notice? What else was happening? That is the project. Not proving anything. Just stopping observations from disappearing.

Why this exists

Auto-urine practice has a long history across multiple traditions, but the structured first-person accounts of what contemporary practitioners actually do and observe have gone largely uncollected. The history and evidence landscape are documented more fully in the founding document. This archive has a simpler purpose: to preserve the observations that otherwise disappear.

My name is Ana. I am a practitioner myself, and I started this project because I got tired of waiting for someone else to do it. Every observation is reviewed by hand. Every interview is a real conversation. This is not a data-harvesting exercise — it is a labour of care, and I take the responsibility of holding people's experiences seriously.

Add an observation

What did you do? What did you notice? What else was changing? That is all an observation needs to be. Two minutes. A few sentences.

You can come back and add more over time. This is a field notebook, not a one-time survey.

  • Add an observation — a short, structured form. Takes two to three minutes.
  • Book a conversation — for experiences that need depth, or if you just want to talk before contributing

You choose how you are identified: real name, pseudonym, or anonymous. Your observation. Your terms.
